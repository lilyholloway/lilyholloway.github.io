Lily Holloway is a writer from Aotearoa who spends too much time on the internet. They received their MFA from Syracuse University where they served as Editor-in-Chief of Salt Hill and received The 2025 Joyce Carol Oates Award in Poetry and The 2025 Joyce Carol Oates Award in Fiction. They are a 2024 winner of the Griffith Review Emerging Voices competition and placed second in the 2024 Black Warrior Review Poetry Competition judged by Donika Kelly. You can find their work published or forthcoming in various places including Sundog Lit, Ōrongohau | Best New Zealand Poems, and Hobart After Dark. They are currently working towards a PhD in Literature & Creative Writing at the University of Houston. Their chapbook was published in 2021 as a part of Auckland University Press' [AUP New Poets 8](/books).

Lily is a Fiction Editor at [Gulf Coast Literary Journal](https://gulfcoastmag.org/).
